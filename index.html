Why Cognit

Stop giving away company data and trade secrets! Your proprietary information does not have to leave your environment to be useful.

When AI touches sensitive work, hosting, security, and data boundaries are part of the product. Cognit helps curate your information, define hardware requirements, install local systems, and integrate leading AI-workflows into your workflows.

Much of what makes a company unique lives in its "tribal knowledge": the deep context, past experience, judgment, and hard-won practices that rarely make it into formal systems. Cognit helps make that rich proprietary knowledge easier to access and apply, so your team can sharpen the competitive edge it has already earned.
